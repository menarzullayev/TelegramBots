# TezMafia — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026.

TezMafia (@qorashahar_mafia_bot) is a Mafia party game bot for Telegram groups. This page explains what the bot collects, why, and when it is deleted.

## What is stored

- Your Telegram user ID, first name and username — when you join a table and while you play.
- Game data: the role you were dealt, votes, night actions and the result.
- Statistics: games played, wins, coin and gem balance, purchased items and a queued role.
- The language chosen for a group or a private chat.
- Payments (Telegram Stars): the payment identifier, amount, date and what was bought. Card or bank details never reach the bot — Telegram processes the payment.
- Technical logs: chat and user IDs, used to fix errors.

The bot does not store ordinary group conversation. Messages removed at night under the game rules are not stored either.

## Why it is used

- To run the game: deal roles, count actions and votes.
- To show statistics, leaderboards and achievements.
- To deliver purchases and handle payment requests.
- To keep the bot stable and prevent abuse.

Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties, except where the law requires it.

## How long it is kept

- Finished games — 90 days, then deleted automatically.
- Technical logs — 14 days.
- Statistics and wallet — until you ask to delete them.
- Payment records — as long as needed for refunds and support requests.

## Your rights

- Send /deletemydata to the bot in a private chat to delete your data. This removes your statistics, wallet, items and language setting, and strips your name and username from finished games.
- Deletion is not possible during an active game; finish the game first.
- For a copy of your data or any other request, write via /paysupport. Requests are answered within 30 days.

## Children

The bot is intended for users old enough to use Telegram under its Terms of Service. If you want your child's data removed, write via /paysupport.

## Security

Data is kept on the bot owner's machine and only the owner can access it. The bot token and the database are never stored in public places.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new text is published on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

Questions and requests: send /paysupport to the bot.

Telegram's general terms for bots also apply: https://telegram.org/privacy-tpa
